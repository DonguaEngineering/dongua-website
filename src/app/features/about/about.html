Who We Are

Practical engineering built around real project delivery

Dongua Engineering Solutions S.L. is a specialist engineering company focused on electrical design for industrial automation. Our work is centred on producing clear, buildable and maintainable electrical designs that integrate effectively within wider engineering projects.

The company brings experience from electrical engineering projects in the UK water and wastewater sector, including control panel and MCC design, motor control, electrical schematics and supporting technical documentation.

We work with system integrators, panel builders and engineering contractors that require specialist electrical engineering support or additional design capacity for their projects.
