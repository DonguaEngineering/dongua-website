My career has combined a strong electrical engineering foundation with practical experience delivering industrial automation projects in the United Kingdom.

After graduating in Electrical Engineering from the University of Málaga, I developed my early automation experience working on electrical design, control panels, HMI and PLC-based systems.

I later specialised in electrical hardware design, supporting water and wastewater projects through the development of MCCs, local control panels, low voltage distribution boards, electrical schematics, general arrangements and engineering documentation.

Dongua Engineering was created to provide that specialist experience directly to system integrators, panel builders and engineering contractors requiring flexible electrical design capacity.
