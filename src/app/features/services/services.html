Our Services

Specialist electrical hardware design for industrial automation

Dongua Engineering provides specialist electrical hardware design for industrial automation projects, supporting system integrators, panel builders and engineering contractors.

Electrical Hardware Design

Control panels, MCCs and low voltage distribution

Electrical designs are developed in accordance with client specifications, engineering standards and project requirements, with a focus on producing clear and practical documentation for manufacture and project delivery.

Design work includes Local Control Panels (LCPs), Motor Control Centres (MCCs) and Low Voltage Distribution Boards (LVDBs), together with the associated electrical schematics and general arrangement drawings.

Design Scope

Electrical design developed around the complete panel system

  • Local Control Panels (LCPs)
  • Motor Control Centres (MCCs)
  • Low Voltage Distribution Boards (LVDBs)
  • Electrical schematics
  • General arrangement drawings
  • Motor control arrangements

Engineering Inputs

Design based on project specifications and engineering data

Electrical designs are developed from the relevant project information and coordinated with the wider engineering documentation.

  • Client specifications and engineering standards
  • Single Line Diagrams (SLDs)
  • Piping and Instrumentation Diagrams (P&IDs)
  • Instrumentation schedules
  • Control philosophy
  • User Requirements Specifications (URS)
  • Load schedules
  • Panel datasheets
